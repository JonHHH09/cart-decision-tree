# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and respect.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, trolling, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct incidents privately through the repository's GitHub Security Advisory channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement ladder](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
